# Problem and Proposed Solution

## A. Problem Definition

Small and medium-sized restaurants must manage different operational and administrative processes, including orders, sales, inventory, purchasing, suppliers, and employee management.

When these processes are managed through separate tools, manual records, or non-integrated systems, information can become fragmented, duplicated, or difficult to update. This can lead to errors, poor inventory control, delays, and difficulties when making decisions.

Therefore, the main problem identified is the lack of integration and centralization of the restaurant's operational and administrative processes.

## B. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system designed for small and medium-sized restaurants.

The system will integrate the main processes of the restaurant into a centralized platform, including:

- Order management
- Sales and billing
- Inventory management
- Purchasing
- Supplier management
- Employee management
- Reports and analytics

The expected product is a centralized ERP platform that facilitates the management and control of restaurant operations, provides organized information, and supports decision-making.

## Main Objective

The main objective is to integrate the restaurant's processes into a single system, reducing information fragmentation and improving operational control.
