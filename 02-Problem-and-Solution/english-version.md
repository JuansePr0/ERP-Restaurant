# English Version – Project Presentation

## Project Context

The project focuses on a small or medium-sized restaurant that provides table service and manages customer orders.

The restaurant must coordinate different processes such as order management, sales, inventory, purchasing, suppliers, and employee management.

When these processes are managed separately, information can become fragmented, duplicated, or difficult to update.

---

## A. Problem Definition

The main problem is the lack of integration and centralization of the restaurant's operational and administrative processes.

Managing orders, sales, inventory, purchases, and suppliers through separate tools or manual records can generate errors, duplicated information, poor inventory control, and difficulties in making timely decisions.

---

## B. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system designed for small and medium-sized restaurants.

The system will integrate the main restaurant processes into a centralized platform, including:

- Order management
- Sales and billing
- Inventory management
- Purchasing
- Supplier management
- Employee management
- Reports and analytics

The expected product is a centralized ERP platform that facilitates restaurant operations, improves information management, and supports decision-making.

---

## C. Justification

The project is valuable because it addresses the need to integrate processes that are directly related to restaurant operations.

The ERP can reduce manual tasks, improve information management, and provide better control over the restaurant's activities.

Existing solutions such as Toast, Lightspeed Restaurant, and Oracle MICROS Simphony demonstrate that integrated restaurant management systems are already relevant in the industry.

However, this project focuses on a controlled-scope solution designed for a small or medium-sized restaurant and developed as an academic software architecture project.

---

## D. Final User

The system is mainly intended for:

- Restaurant administrators
- Cashiers
- Waiters
- Kitchen staff
- Inventory and purchasing personnel

Each user will have access to the functions required according to their role.

---

## E. Utility and Return on Investment

The potential return on investment can be achieved by reducing waste, manual errors, and the time spent on repetitive tasks.

Better inventory and purchasing control can also help reduce unnecessary expenses and improve the restaurant's operational efficiency.

---

## ERP Advantages

- Centralized information
- Integration between processes
- Better inventory control
- Reduction of manual errors
- Easier access to reports
- Process optimization
- Support for decision-making

## ERP Disadvantages

- Initial implementation costs
- Employee training requirements
- Resistance to change
- Maintenance and updates
- Dependence on technological infrastructure
- Complexity of system integration

---

## Existing Solutions

The project analyzed three existing restaurant management platforms:

### Toast

Toast provides restaurant POS, inventory, purchasing, supplier, employee management, and reporting capabilities.

### Lightspeed Restaurant

Lightspeed Restaurant provides POS, order management, inventory, employee management, kitchen operations, and reporting features.

### Oracle MICROS Simphony

Oracle MICROS Simphony provides POS, inventory, employee management, kitchen operations, menu management, and analytics.

---

## Project Differentiation

The objective is not to replace commercial solutions such as Toast, Lightspeed, or Oracle MICROS Simphony.

Instead, the project proposes a controlled-scope ERP focused on the essential processes of a small or medium-sized restaurant.

The project also provides an opportunity to apply software architecture concepts to a real-world business context.
